# Research foundations

English · [繁體中文](research-foundations.zh-TW.md)

Reviewed 2026-11-11. This is a curated design bibliography, not a systematic review. Sources were selected for comprehension, author control, revision, cross-media communication and evaluation. The application column is a **Storytelling design inference**, not a result established for this project. No paper establishes that this skill improves writing in every language or medium.

Use the [rules](editorial-rules.md) and [workflows](research-workflows.md) to apply this material. Read only the sources relevant to the task.

| P01 · Zwaan, Langston & Graesser (1995), *The Construction of Situation Models in Narrative Comprehension: An Event-Indexing Model*. Psychological Science. IPOCL models causal progression and character goals; its evaluation addresses comprehension of character intentions. The arXiv deposit is from 2014; the journal paper is from 2010. | Separate character goals, author intent and audience knowledge. Use a goal-oriented profile only when appropriate. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.06796) · [project](https://coauthor.stanford.edu/) | Project description and paper abstract. The dataset records 63 writers and 1,445 English sessions, including requests, acceptance, dismissal and editing of suggestions. | Preserve proposals and author decisions separately. | P05 · Masson, Zhao & Chevalier (2025), *Visual Story-Writing: Writing by Manipulating Visual Representations of Stories*. UIST. Two prototype studies examined planning/reviewing (12 participants) and creative writing (8). Visuals helped participants locate and track story elements. Study 1 did not establish a significant cognitive-load reduction. | Offer an impact map and inspect actual revised text. Do not impose this overhead on a short anecdote or transfer the paper's effect sizes to this skill. |

Source existence is distinct from support. Our manual claim table is not an implementation of FActScore and is not its benchmark score. |
| P08 · Amershi et al. (2019), *Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction*. CHI. Eighteen guidelines were assessed through several rounds, including 49 practitioners working with 20 AI products. | Make limits visible and support correction and dismissal. This is interaction-design guidance, not evidence of literary quality. |
| P09 · Mayer & Moreno (2003), *Nine Ways to Reduce Cognitive Load in Multimedia Learning*. Educational Psychologist 38(1), 43–52. [Publisher](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1207/S15326985EP3801_6) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1207/S15326985EP3801_6) | Publisher abstract retrieved through search; direct page access returned 403. The paper discusses limited processing capacity and overload in multimedia instruction. | Review competing narration, visual information and sound for educational work. | P10 · Zheng et al. (2023), *Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena*. NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks. [Paper, v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685v4) | Author abstract. ## What these sources do not justify

- Do not require three acts, a hero's journey, a conflict, a happy ending or a fixed emotional curve for every work. These can be named, optional craft profiles.
- Do not infer audience emotion from a sentiment score. Do not infer comprehension from the presence of required fields.
- Do not equate chronological order, reveal order and execution prerequisites. Only the last must be a DAG in the existing workflow planner.
- Do not infer that Rust cannot support a medium because no renderer exists. Separate representable contracts, implemented commands and external production capabilities.
- Do not import paper text, illustrations, datasets, model weights or reference code merely because the citation is public. This package contains original summaries and synthetic examples. Check the specific upstream license before any future import.

## Add or revise an evidence entry

Record title, authors, publication year, venue, primary URL or DOI, inspected sections, access limits, original task/population, applicable rule, counterexample and a local evaluation. Mark a theory, a design-space study, an empirical result, a standard and an engineering analogy distinctly. Keep contradictory findings. A more recent paper does not automatically supersede a better-matched study.

Research can justify a testable question. Only project observations can establish that the proposed workflow helps its intended users.
