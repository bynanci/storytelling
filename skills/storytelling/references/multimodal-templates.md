# Multimodal templates · 多模態創作樣板

## Overview

Pick the section that matches the deliverable. Copy either language prompt, replace its brackets, and give it to Storytelling. The worked examples below share one fictional source packet. You can use them without files or a media editor. If you supply real files, record which ones the host could inspect and which details came from your own descriptions.

依交付物選一節，複製中文或英文樣板，把方括號換成自己的資料。每節都有兩種寫法或設計方法，可挑一種直接使用，也可要求各出一版。下表是可直接試作的虛構素材，描述已提供，無須另找檔案。

| ID | Sample material | 試作素材 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| N1 | Notice for the next repair table. Saturday, 14:00–16:00, in the courtyard. Bring an umbrella. Walk-ins welcome. | 下次修傘桌公告。週六 14:00–16:00，中庭。請帶一把傘，無須預約。 |
| P1 | Still from a previous session. One person holds an umbrella handle on the left. Another sorts tools on the right. | 上次活動的照片。左側一人握著傘柄，右側另一人整理工具。 |
| V1 | Four-second silent clip from the previous session. A hand presses the catch, then the canopy opens. | 上次活動的四秒無聲片段。一隻手按下傘扣，傘面隨後展開。 |
| A1 | Two-second recording of two short clicks. Its source and timing relative to V1 are unknown. | 兩秒錄音中有兩聲短促喀聲。發聲物與它相對於 V1 的時間皆未知。 |

For hands-on practice with files you can open, use the separate [downloadable source packet](workshop-demo.md). Its notice, drawing and sound have their own contents. Keep the 14:00 start, people in the photo and four-second clip within the N1/P1/V1/A1 exercise above.

要練習讀取真實檔案，可另用[可下載的多模態素材包](workshop-demo.md)，分別查看公告、圖稿與聲音。上表 14:00 的開始時間、照片中的兩人及四秒影片只屬於 N1／P1／V1／A1 描述練習。引用下載素材時，請依檔案實際呈現的細節撰寫。

A screenplay scene, illustration brief, cue sheet and shot list are authoring outputs. Ask for a rendered image, audio file or edited video separately when the host offers that tool. A draft can describe a new sound or image and name it as a proposal.

劇本、構圖稿、聲音提示表和剪輯表都可直接以文字交付。若要實際的圖檔、錄音或成片，請另指定產物與工具。成品文字下方可用一行註明素材、推論或創作提案，讓觀眾看到的內容保持自然。

## Screenplay

**編劇 · Scene or short film.** Choose social realism to follow a visible action as it unfolds. Choose a flashback structure to delay what the audience knows. A change of style should alter the reveal order or dramatic choice.

> 用［素材］為［觀眾］寫［場數與長度］的戲。採［社會寫實／倒敘揭露］。給場景標頭、可演的動作與角色做出的選擇，再列事件順序及觀眾得知的順序。自行加的人物、台詞或動機放在創作備註。

> Write [number and length of scenes] for [audience] from [source]. Use [social realism / a flashback reveal]. Give scene headings, playable action and a character choice. List event order and reveal order. Mark invented characters, dialogue or motives in a production note.

**示例 / Treatment.** 寫實版從 V1 的動作出發。編劇可以讓工作人員把傘柄轉向訪客，讓他自己按。倒敘版先看見傘面展開，第二場才揭露有人把傘交到另一人手上。*In both versions the handover is dramatized. Only the press and opening come from V1.* 驗收時，演員須能照動作表演，讀者也能指出哪個瞬間是編劇加的。

## Illustration

**插畫與海報 · Single-image concept.** An International Typographic Style treatment uses a grid, alignment and hierarchy to help someone read a notice in passing. A cut-paper collage can use overlap and shifts of scale to draw attention to people working together.

> 為［觀看者與使用位置］依［素材］出［比例］的兩款構圖。A 用國際主義平面設計的網格，B 用剪紙拼貼。各寫焦點、前後景、字區、視線路徑與一項刻意省略的細節。標出新添的視覺元素。

> Design two [aspect ratio] compositions for [viewer and placement] using [sources]. Use a typographic grid for A and cut-paper collage for B. Specify the focal point, depth, text area, reading path, one omission and any invented visual element.

**示例 / Treatment.** A 左上放 N1 的「週六 14:00」，右側直欄只裁 P1 的傘柄，讀者先看時段再看地點。B 放大 P1 兩個人的手部剪影，在它們之間疊一片提議的靛藍圓紙，右下留白放公告。*A leads with time. B leads with the gesture and introduces a paper shape.* 縮成手機縮圖後，A 應仍可辨認時間，B 應仍可分辨兩個人物的動作。

## Copy

**商業文案 · Announcement or campaign.** Use AIDA (attention, interest, desire, action) for a reader encountering an offer for the first time. Use a problem–solution structure when readers already recognize the need. Ask for evidence before turning an attractive inference into a claim.

> 用［公告／產品事實及來源］，替［受眾、通路］寫［字數］文案。各出 AIDA 與「問題→解法」一版，交付主標、內文與一個可做的下一步。把每項事實主張對回來源，列出仍缺佐證的用語。

> Using [facts and sources], write [length] of copy for [audience and channel]. Give an AIDA version and a problem–solution version, each with a headline, body and one concrete action. Map factual claims to evidence and identify any wording that needs support.

**示例 / Draft lines.** AIDA 可用「週六，把那把傘帶下樓」開場，再給 N1 的地點、時間與參與方式。問題導向版先答「想請人看看你的傘？週六 14:00–16:00 帶到中庭，無須預約。」*The first opens with an invitation, the second answers a practical question.* 「免費」和「一定修好」均缺來源，成稿保留已知的參加方式。驗收時確認讀者能從文案知道何時、去哪裡、帶什麼。

## Brainstorm

**腦力激盪 · Alternatives and a decision.** Use SCAMPER prompts to change an existing format, such as reversing who demonstrates. Use reverse brainstorming to imagine how participation could fail, then turn a specific failure into a design option.

> 以［現況］與［目標、限制］發想。用 SCAMPER 改一個既有做法，再用逆向腦力激盪找一個會讓參與失敗的原因。每條交付構想、可能奏效的理由、待查問題與最小試做。依［判準］收斂，保留另一條及其適用條件。

> Given [current setup], [goal] and [constraints], change one element with SCAMPER. Use reverse brainstorming to find one reason participation might fail. For each branch, give an idea, rationale, unknown and small trial. Compare using [criterion] and retain the alternative with its conditions.

**示例 / Decision.** 若目標是「路過的人敢開口」，SCAMPER 的「反轉」可讓訪客先示範傘在哪裡卡住。逆向提問「怎樣讓人完全不敢問？」得到「每個人都得當眾講」，由此設計可指選的故障圖卡。*Pilot one spoken invitation and one picture card. Count visitors who approach and those who describe or point out a problem.* 兩者都是待試方案，尚無觀察結果時保留兩條分支。

## Everyday

**生活敘事 · Personal retelling or practical message.** Open on a scene for a friend, or put the answer first for someone who needs to decide whether to go. Choose a viewpoint the supplied material supports.

> 用［親歷細節／我拿到的素材］向［對象］講一段［長度］的事。先出「場景開場」口語版，再出「重點先講」訊息版。保留［不可動的事實］，並用符合我實際所見的視角各寫一句開頭。

> Tell [listener] a [length] story using [firsthand details or supplied notes]. Write one scene-first spoken version and one summary-first message. Keep [fixed facts] and use a viewpoint consistent with how I learned this.

**示例 / Opening lines.** 給朋友：「照片裡，左邊的人握著傘柄，右邊的人在理工具。兩個人各忙一半。」回覆想參加的家人：「週六兩點到四點，帶傘到中庭就行，無須預約。」*The friend gets a scene from P1. The family gets practical details from N1.* 把第一段讀出聲，檢查敘述者有沒有把「看到照片」說成「我當時就在現場」。

## Audio

**聲音敘事 · Podcast opening or listening piece.** An observational sound diary foregrounds what was recorded. A narrated essay can pair a voice with the recording as an editorial motif. Both can leave silence for the listener to interpret.

> 用［錄音及長度］與［文字來源］設計［秒數］音訊開頭。選［觀察式聲音日誌／旁白散文］，交付時間、聲軌、口播原文、停頓與收尾提示。每個 cue 標記實錄、預計錄製或擬音，另外列可供讀稿的逐字文字。

> Plan a [duration] audio opening from [recordings with durations] and [written sources]. Use [observational sound diary / narrated essay]. Give timed tracks, exact voice lines, pauses and an ending cue. Mark recorded, to-be-recorded and proposed Foley, and provide a readable transcript.

**示例 / Ten-second cue sheet.**

| Time | Sound diary / 聲音日誌 | Narrated essay / 旁白散文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 s | Play A1 alone. 原錄音播放兩聲喀聲。 | Voice to record: “Listen twice.” 待錄旁白：「聽兩下。」 |
| 2–4 s | Hold silence. 留白兩秒。 | Play A1 alone as a motif. 讓兩聲喀聲獨立出現。 |
| 4–10 s | Voice to record: “Two clicks. The recording ends there.” 待錄：「喀、喀。錄音就停在這裡。」 | Voice to record: “Saturday afternoon, bring your umbrella to the courtyard.” 待錄：「週六下午，帶把傘來中庭。」 |

The transcript identifies A1 as two clicks. Keep their cause open and place them on their own track when adapting to picture. 驗收時確認十秒無空漏、停頓有標示、待錄旁白已有文字稿，聽者不會被引導認定喀聲出自 V1 的傘扣。

## Video

**影片 · Shot plan.** Continuity editing follows an action so viewers can read it. Associative montage places images beside each other to suggest a relationship. Keep the distinction between source time and the proposed timeline.

> 依［素材 ID、來源區間、是否有聲］做［秒數］鏡頭表。各出順接剪輯與聯想蒙太奇。每列標成片起迄、素材、動作或轉場、聲音來源與缺件。指定要交付的［鏡頭表／已支援的結構化影片輸入］。

> Make a [duration] shot list from [asset IDs, source ranges and audio status]. Give continuity and associative-montage treatments. For each shot, name output time, source, action or cut, sound source and missing material. Deliver [a textual shot plan / supported structured video input].

**示例 / Two eight-second plans.** 順接版在成片 0–4 秒完整播放 V1 的 0–4 秒，4–8 秒顯示 N1 製作的公告字卡。聯想版 0–2 秒取 P1 裁切的雙手，2–6 秒接 V1 全段，6–8 秒回到靜態傘柄。兩版均可保持無聲。*The first ends with an invitation. The second ends on the hands’ shared work.* 第二版若加 A1，須標為剪輯配聲。原片沒有同步聲軌。計畫應可加總為八秒，字卡與照片的停留時間屬於成片時間。[Structured video handoff](video-cli.md)

## Mixed

**跨模態組合 · A single story across channels.** Give each channel a job. A public-information treatment leads with a visitor's next action. A poetic treatment can pair sound and image through rhythm while keeping time, place and evidence legible wherever the brief requires them.

> 以［共用素材及 ID］為［受眾與用途］製作［交付物、數量與篇幅］。選［資訊導向／詩意拼貼］，指定每項交付物各自負責的內容，直接寫出草案，再給一張版本與來源對照表。若［某一資訊］改變，只改引用它的輸出。列出尚缺的檔案或授權。

> Use [shared sources with IDs] for [audience and purpose]. Produce [formats, counts and lengths] using [public information / poetic collage]. Assign each format a job, deliver each draft, then map variants to sources. Revise the outputs that use [one changed fact]. List missing assets or permissions.

**示例 / A courtyard invitation.** Prepare an entrance poster, a voice line for a community audio feed and an atmospheric video loop for people already at the table.

| Output | Worked draft / 可直接審稿的草案 | Uses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poster / 入口海報 | 「週六修傘桌｜14:00–16:00 中庭｜帶傘即可，無須預約。」在字區下方裁入握傘與整理工具的雙手。The poster answers when, where and what to bring. | N1, P1 |
| Voice / 社區音訊口播 | 「週六下午兩點到四點，帶把傘到中庭。修傘桌不用預約。」Read aloud once to check the pace. | N1 |
| Loop / 桌邊無聲循環 | 0–2 秒看 P1 左邊的手，2–6 秒播放 V1，6–8 秒回到 P1 右邊的工具，循環點切回左手。Let the hands carry the story. | P1, V1 |

**When the attachments arrive / 收到實際附件時。** A producer attaches the original N1 notice, P1 photo and A1 recording and asks for a phone poster and a short audio teaser. Read the notice, inspect the image and listen to the recording separately when the host supports each action. Record the input status before composing.

| Evidence label | Example entry / 可核對的註記 |
| --- | --- |
| Observed / 已檢視 | After reading N1, transcribe “14:00–16:00”. After opening P1, describe the left hand on the umbrella handle. If A1 plays, mark two audible clicks at the recording level. |
| Reported / 作者提供 | 若音檔開不了，記「作者形容 A1 有兩聲喀聲，尚未試聽」。若連描述也沒有，留「A1 待試聽」。先交海報文字、口播稿與預留的聲音 cue。 |
| Inferred / 詮釋 | P1 的雙手可讀為「合作」。把它用作海報構圖方向。照片無從證明兩人彼此認識、傘已修好或聲音來自傘扣。 |
| Proposed / 創作提案 | Let the audio teaser open on A1 and pause before the N1 invitation. A poetic alternative answers the two clicks with two spoken words. Mark the echo as a new voice cue. |

Check one claim in each output against its cited source. Deliver the image or audio file only after the appropriate tool has produced a file and the result has been inspected or played back. Otherwise name the delivered brief or cue sheet and the missing step. 若 A1 無法播放，海報可先定稿。實際音訊交付仍需可聽的檔案與回聽結果。

**Revision check.** N1 的開始時間若改為 15:00，海報與口播都要改，桌邊循環沿用原剪法。若只有 A1 的聲源辨識有誤，這三件作品都沒有引用 A1。*The same source correction has a different reach depending on which deliverables actually use it.* 若這次僅提供上表的素材描述，交付的是字稿與製作規劃，真正的圖檔、錄音、影片與使用許可仍需另備。
