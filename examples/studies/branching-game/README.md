# The ferry token

[繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [All cases](../../README.md)

## Audience and author request

First-time players reading two versions of the same ferry scene, one beat at a time; they share the same knowledge when they start.

Write two playable text scenes in three beats from the supplied world notes. Give both routes the same events and ending. Change when the player learns Mara's reason. Keep Ivo's knowledge consistent with what he sees. Give the quest editor a third card to hand to QA.

## Source cards

| ID | Material |
| --- | --- |
| S1 | At the first bell Mara slips the brass token from the ferry hook into her sleeve. Ivo arrives, touches the empty hook and calls for the keeper. |
| S2 | Mara's folded note reads, “I borrowed the token to get Dad across. I'll bring it back.” Ivo only opens it at the second bell. A player may see it before Ivo does. His thumb comes to rest on the word “Dad.” |
| P1 | For the early reveal only, frame the note as an inset close-up over the pier. This is a proposed screen treatment. |

Reveal one beat at a time. Record what the player believes the character knows before showing the next beat.
