When I was late for a family meal, what I dreaded was the moment everyone might stop for me. That day the feeling left me as a bowl of soup came into my hands. If you look at the photograph from 1996, you'll see my aunt and me between two wooden chairs, both still touching the rim of the white bowl. The camera kept our hands. It couldn't keep whatever we said, and I don't remember the words now. What stayed with me was the feeling that I could sit down with the others.
