Look at the hands on the rim of that white bowl. For the instant the camera caught in 1996, my aunt and I were both holding it. In the photograph the two wooden chairs frame us. What you can't see is how late I was, or how much I hated the idea of making everyone stop for me. I remember the bowl coming toward me. I don't remember anybody's exact words. I do remember that, by the time I had the soup in my hands, I felt I could sit down with the others.
