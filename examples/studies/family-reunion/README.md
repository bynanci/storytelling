# The bowl between the chairs

[繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [All cases](../../README.md)

## Audience and author request

Relatives at a reunion listening to a roughly 45-second family toast; each listener hears both candidate takes without seeing the source photograph.

Write two approximately 45-second spoken toasts for the same family reunion, using one synthetic old photo and a narrator's recollection. One voice can begin with a visible gesture and the other with what it came to mean. Paraphrase remembered events and keep precise dialogue out. Give the slide operator a standalone cue.

## Source cards

| ID | Material |
| --- | --- |
| S1 | The photo is dated 1996. Between two wooden chairs, an aunt holds a white bowl of soup toward a younger relative. Both hands still touch its rim. The photo captures that instant only. |
| S2 | The younger relative, now the narrator, remembers arriving late, worrying about interrupting and feeling welcome when handed soup. There is no recording or reliable memory of anyone's exact words. |
| P1 | For the projection only, the slide operator has proposed starting with a close crop of the hands, then widening to the two chairs. This is a presentation choice. |
