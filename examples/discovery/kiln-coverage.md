# The first look inside the kiln

[繁體中文](kiln-coverage.zh-TW.md) · [Scenario index](../README.md)

An assistant editor needs two decisions from the director. Which shot opens the sequence, and which moment still needs footage? A and B are written edit briefs for the same director. C is a missing-shot note for the producer. The sources and drafts are fictional and intended for a text reading. The video coverage CLI requires a separate JSON input.

## Sources for the author

| Card | Source | Evidence limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | The potter's log says the door was closed before firing. The next day the potter opened it at 09:10, then put the blue bowl on the workbench. | The log dates the event. It does not say whether a camera recorded it. |
| S2 | The delivery lists two clips. K1 shows the door closing before firing. K2 shows the blue bowl on the workbench. This delivery has no shot of the door opening. | The list covers this delivery. The bowl on the workbench provides no shot of the door opening. |
| P1 | For the version that shows the finished bowl first, the assistant editor proposes the title “Out of the kiln” over the bowl. | The director has yet to decide. |

## Drafts to share separately

### A

> Open on the kiln door closing. K1 gives us that shot. The log puts the opening at 09:10 the next day. We still need footage for that beat. Then show the blue bowl on the workbench. K2 gives us the finished piece.

### B

> Open on the blue bowl on the workbench. K2 gives us the finished piece. Go back to the kiln door closing. K1 gives us that shot. The log puts the opening at 09:10 the next day. We still need footage for that beat. For your review, I suggest the title “Out of the kiln” over the bowl.

### C

> We need a shot of the instant the kiln door opens. Please check whether other footage already covers it. If the search comes up empty, ask the director whether to schedule a pickup.

## Two change cards

For each attempt, start with all three original drafts. Give the editor one card and collect the full text of A, B and C.

- **R1**　The potter corrects the opening time in the log to 09:20. The delivery list stays the same.
- **R2**　The assistant editor withdraws the “Out of the kiln” title proposal. The director still wants the finished piece first in that version.

## Facilitator key

Share only the quoted text of A or B under a neutral name. First ask “Which image came to mind first? Which words prompted it?” Collect the answer before asking “Which shots could you cut with the available material? What, if anything, would you check with the producer first?” Expect the closing door first for A and the bowl on the workbench first for B. Separately record whether the reader identifies the missing opening shot. Keep the reader's words if they say K2 shows the opening or infer from the gap that the kiln never opened that day. The briefs also differ in their proposed title. Align that detail before a trial intended to isolate order.

A uses S1 and S2. B uses S1, S2 and P1. C uses S2.

| Card | Required edit | What to protect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Update the log time in A and B to 09:20. | Return C verbatim. K1 and K2 still cover only the shots on the delivery list. |
| R2 | Remove only the title proposal from B. | Keep the bowl first in B. Return A and C verbatim. The pickup decision stays open. |
