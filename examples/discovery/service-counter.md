# Which channel can I use to book a court?

English · [繁體中文](service-counter.zh-TW.md)

A municipal sports centre issues a court-booking notice at 11 a.m. Residents need to know where they can submit a new booking and where they can check an existing one. The desk also needs to brief the next shift. This exercise uses fictional records.

## Source cards

| Card | Source | Evidence limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | The website service log says that online booking reopened at 10:20 a.m. today. | This time applies to the website only. |
| S2 | The desk log says callers can check existing bookings. New bookings by phone remain suspended. This shift issued three enquiry receipts. | The log gives no enquiry outcomes or count of distinct people. |
| P1 | The shift supervisor wants a field at the end of the handover note for any receipt numbers that need a second look. | The individual records determine whether any need review. |

## Reading A

To book a court, you can now submit a new booking on the website. You can call to check an existing booking. New bookings by phone remain suspended. Online booking reopened at 10:20 a.m. today.

## Reading B

Online booking reopened at 10:20 a.m. today. To book a court, you can now submit a new booking on the website. You can call to check an existing booking. New bookings by phone remain suspended.

## C · Shift handover note

This shift issued three enquiry receipts. Callers can check existing bookings. New bookings by phone remain suspended.

Receipt numbers to review, if any ____

## Change cards

- **R1**　S1 corrects the time when online booking reopened to 10:35 a.m.
- **R2**　The supervisor withdraws the request in P1 for a field listing receipt numbers to review.

## Facilitator notes

Show a first-time reader only the text of one notice. Ask “What did you notice first? Which words led you there?” Then ask “How would you book a court now? Which sentence guided you?” Record the answers in the reader's own words before showing the other version.

- **Blind reading.** In A the expected first focus is the option to book online. In B it is the time when online booking reopened. Also record whether the reader picks a working channel for a new booking. “I can book online and call to check an existing booking” is supported. “Both channels started taking bookings at 10:20” extends the website time and service to the phone.
- **Source map.** A and B cite S1 and S2. C cites S2 and P1. Start each change card from the originals and collect all three drafts.
- **R1.** Change 10:20 to 10:35 in A and B. Return C as supplied. Changing only B misses A. Adding “phone bookings reopened at 10:35” to C also damages its account of what callers can do.
- **R2.** Remove only the empty field from C. Keep the three enquiry receipts and the scope of phone service. Three receipts support neither “three people booked successfully” nor “all three enquiries need review.”
