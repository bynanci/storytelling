# SK-E04 · Anchored judging rubric v1

This rubric is for a separately versioned calibration run. It must not be substituted into the SK-E04 order-only run, which requires the original rubric unchanged. Score each dimension independently from 1 to 5. Scores 2 and 4 are intermediate between the adjacent anchors. Keep factual accuracy, narrative quality and task coverage separate.

| Dimension | 1 · serious failure | 3 · mixed / partial | 5 · strong | 中文錨點 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Factual accuracy | Material claim is contradicted, invented, or loses a qualifier in a way that changes meaning. | Main facts are usable, but one non-central claim or qualifier is uncertain or imprecise. | All material claims match the available record and retain scope and qualifiers. | 1：主要主張與來源矛盾、捏造或因漏限定而改變意思。3：主要事實可用，但有一項次要說法或限定不精確。5：重要主張均符合可用紀錄並保留範圍與限定。 |
| Source traceability | Material claims cannot be linked to a source or are assigned to the wrong speaker. | Most important claims are attributed, with a visible gap or imprecise location. | Each material claim links to the correct source revision and speaker; unavailable evidence remains labeled. | 1：重要主張無法追溯，或歸錯來源／說話者。3：多數重要主張可追溯，但仍有明確缺口。5：重要主張連到正確來源修訂與說話者；缺件仍明示。 |

## Scoring rules

- Freeze the task and required/optional field list before generation or judging.
- Do not penalize an optional omission as factual error or task failure. A missing required field is a coverage omission; a false replacement is a factual error. Record both when both occur.
- Use W4 states `supported`, `contradicted`, `insufficient` and `unavailable` in a separate source adjudication record. A source link or citation alone is not evidence of support.
- A fluent or coherent draft can still be factually wrong. Never collapse dimensions into a single quality score for acceptance.
- Preserve the original raw rating and rationale. A later adjudication is a second field with its reason and reviewer; do not overwrite the first judgment.
- Treat this rubric as a new instrument. Any run using it is separate from the order-only run and from the historical R1 scores.
