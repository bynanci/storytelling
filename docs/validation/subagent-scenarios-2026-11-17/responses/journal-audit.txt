## Meaningful regression

`nonconsecutive-readback-replay.rs` is a test sketch to append to the existing
integration-test module. It replays a NotApplied receipt after a later Ambiguous
receipt, captures both the replay snapshot and attempted retry, and checks full
history plus the persisted readback count. The parent reviewer can execute it
before any fix in its own explicitly assigned Cargo target. A companion scenario
should show four unique ambiguous readbacks remain admissible when older exact
receipts are replayed between them, including after reopen.

The same latest-only design also means replaying an original response after a
readback with another digest returns StaleAttempt, and replaying an older
ambiguous readback can consume budget or return LimitExceeded after exhaustion.
These are consequences of the receipt-history issue, not independent Host claims.

## Other inspected boundaries

Identity construction freezes every accepted field and length-frames the intent
fingerprint. An exact prepare replay is a no-op, while changed identity under
either unique key rejects. Mutating lifecycle methods begin IMMEDIATE
transactions; lease/fence checks and current-attempt checks precede duplicate
shortcuts. Exact expiry is rejected by `until <= now_ms`; a reclaimed dispatch
marker becomes unknown in the same transaction as the new owner/fence. Returned
errors after writes roll the transaction back through the transaction guard.
Attempt/readback limits and independent-operation availability have existing
tests. No additional blocker was identified in those inspected paths.

Clock trust and monotonic wall time are explicitly outside accepted V1: callers
supply nonnegative epoch times and the journal does not prevent wall-clock skew
(`M3-02-OPERATION-JOURNAL.md:99`–`:102`). `renew` assigns now+duration and can
shorten an existing expiry; that is a documentation/semantics question, not the
reported failure and not evidence of a missing trusted-clock implementation.
